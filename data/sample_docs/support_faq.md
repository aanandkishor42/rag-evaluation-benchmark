# Support FAQ - NimbusWorks

## Refunds
- Self-serve refunds are available for 30 days after the purchase date.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- Subscriptions cancelled after the refund window stay active until the end of the billing period.

## Accounts and Access
- Reset your password from the login page with the "Forgot password" link.
- Password reset links expire after 24 hours.
- Account support can unlock accounts blocked by repeated failed logins.
- Permanent account deletion removes all projects and is irreversible.

## Data and Privacy
- After an account is cancelled, stored data is retained for 90 days before deletion.
- Data processed by the AI assistant is not used to train third-party models.
- API keys rotate instantly: revoking a key stops its access immediately.
- Users can export all of their projects in JSON or CSV format at any time.

## Billing Queries
- VAT invoices are available in the billing settings under "Tax documents".
- Failed payments trigger a 7-day grace period before service suspension.
