# Employee Handbook - NimbusWorks

## Leave and Time Off
- Full-time employees accrue 22 days of annual paid leave.
- Leave rolls over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.
- Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid, available after 6 months of employment.

## Remote Work
- NimbusWorks is a remote-first company. Employees may work from anywhere.
- Working abroad for more than 4 consecutive weeks requires manager approval.
- The company reimburses co-working space costs up to $150 per month.

## Onboarding
- New hires complete security training during their first week.
- All employees must set up SSO and two-factor authentication on day one.
- Access grants are reviewed once per quarter by the IT team.

## Expense Policy
- Expense reimbursements are processed within 14 days of submission.
- Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of the purchase date.
- The manager's prior approval is required for purchases over $300.

## Code of Conduct
- Discrimination and harassment are strictly prohibited.
- All communications on company channels are subject to the public records policy.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the People team in writing.
