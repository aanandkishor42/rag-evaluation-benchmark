# Product Pricing - NimbusWorks Platform

## Plans
- Free: 1 project, 1 user, 100 AI queries per month.
- Pro: $29 per user per month. Includes 5 projects, unlimited viewers, the AI
  assistant, and 10,000 document pages processed per month through the API.
- Business: $79 per user per month. Includes everything in Pro, plus unlimited
  projects, single sign-on (SSO), priority support, and audit logs.

## Billing
- Billing is per user, prorated by the day for mid-month changes.
- Annual billing is charged for 10 months, effectively 2 months free.
- Invoices are generated on the first day of the month and paid via card or ACH.

## Limits and Fair Use
- The Free plan API limit is 100 queries per month and resets on renewal.
- Business plan API processing can be raised to 100,000 pages per month on request.
- Automation tools (Zapier, Make) count each task as one AI query.

## Add-ons
- Custom data connectors start at $199 per month.
- Dedicated success manager is available on Business and above at no extra cost.
- On-premise or VPC deployment is available for enterprise contracts.
